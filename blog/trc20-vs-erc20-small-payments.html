TRC-20 vs ERC-20 for small USDT payments

If you're accepting $3–$50 payments, the network choice matters more than the payment provider's fee schedule.

USDT on TRON (TRC-20)USDT on Ethereum (ERC-20)
Transfer costSender typically pays a small TRX energy/bandwidth cost — usually centsGas varies with congestion — can spike far above a $3 order's value
Confirmation~3s blocks; usable within seconds~12s slots; comfortable settlement takes a couple of minutes
Wallet supportUniversal in TRON-aware wallets and exchangesUniversal, the default everywhere
Best formicro-payments, invoices, high-frequency checkoutlarge transfers where a few dollars of gas is irrelevant

The catch with TRC-20

Receiving is cheap; sending from a fresh wallet costs energy or TRX. For a merchant this rarely matters — customers send to you, you hold. Also, some exchanges charge notable withdrawal fees for USDT-TRC-20 on-ramps, so check your off-ramp too.

Practical recommendation

For orders under ~$100: TRC-20. The buyer's cost stays predictable, your watcher sees confirmations fast, and the 6-decimal amount trick works identically either way — the chain doesn't care which network carries your order ID.
