How to accept USDT without a payment gateway

Payment gateways charge 0.5–1%, hold funds, run KYC, and sometimes just refuse your country. But USDT on TRON is a public ledger — anyone can read it. You don't need a middleman to notice a payment. You need a way to identify which order a transfer belongs to, and a way to watch your address.

Step 1 — the 6-decimal trick

USDT (TRC-20) has 6 decimals. If your product costs $5, don't ask for 5.000000 — generate a unique amount per order, like 5.003714. The fractional digits become your order ID. Any incoming transfer now uniquely identifies its order — no per-order wallet generation, no extra gas.

amount = price + random(1000..9999) / 1_000_000

Collision chance with a handful of live orders is negligible, and a single-use tx_hash set stops replays.

Step 2 — watch the chain

TRON exposes free endpoints. TronGrid's account-transfers API needs no key at low volumes:

GET https://api.trongrid.io/v1/accounts/YOUR_ADDRESS/transactions/trc20?only_to=true&limit=50

Every minute (cron), pull incoming transfers, filter for the USDT contract (TR7NHqjeKQxGTCi8q8ZY4pL8otSzgjLj6t), and compare value / 1e6 against your pending orders. Match + order created before the block timestamp → mark paid.

Step 3 — edge cases you must handle

  • Exact match only. Never round. Underpays stay pending.
  • Timestamp guard. Only accept transfers after the order was created (old txs can't pay new orders).
  • One tx = one order. Keep a seen:{tx_hash} record with a long TTL.
  • Expiry. Kill unpaid orders after 30 minutes so amounts can be reused safely.
  • Finality. TRON blocks are ~3s; for small amounts a couple of confirmations is plenty.

What this gives you

  • 0% platform fee — you pay zero to nobody (network cost is on the sender for TRC-20 transfers)
  • Funds land directly in your own wallet — non-custodial by construction
  • No KYC, no country restrictions, no account to freeze

The trade-off: you own the reliability. The watcher must actually run — a dead cron means delayed confirmations. Put it on something with a schedule (Cloudflare Workers cron is free) and alert yourself on payment.
